InTab PDF is a simple app for reading and managing PDF files on your Android device.

All PDF processing happens on your device. Your documents are not uploaded to any server. You can use the app offline without an internet connection.

What you can do with InTab PDF:

• PDF Reader: Open and read PDF files with text search, zoom controls and page thumbnails. Use distraction-free mode for comfortable reading.

• Merge PDF: Combine multiple PDF documents into one file and arrange them in your preferred order.

• Rotate PDF: Rotate all pages or selected pages in 90-degree steps.

• Remove Pages: Delete unwanted pages or page ranges.

• Split PDF: Extract selected page ranges or split a document into smaller files.

• Images to PDF: Convert JPG, PNG and WebP images into PDF. Arrange images and choose A4 or fit-to-page sizing.

• Compress PDF: Reduce PDF file size using a target size or compression presets.

• More tools are coming soon.

Privacy:

• No account or login required.
• No ads, analytics, telemetry or tracking.
• No backend server or cloud uploads.
• Your files stay on your device unless you choose to save or share them.
